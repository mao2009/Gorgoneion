# Funding and Commercialization Policy

## Independent development

Gorgoneion begins as an independently developed, free-to-use project. GitHub Sponsors may help fund engineering, testing, research and infrastructure. Sponsorship offers no uptime SLA, guaranteed fixes, or preferential emergency response.

## Potential transition to a company

When the product and its customer base justify it, the maintainer intends to establish a company and introduce **paid plans at or around incorporation**. This is a plan, **not a deadline or a promise of commercialization**. Free distribution may continue if sustainable.

Prices, scope, licensing, upgrade paths, and support terms will be publicly announced before any change. Existing grants under published OSS licenses will remain valid; any later relicensing requires rights to all relevant code and compliance with upstream licenses.

## Sustainability gates

Assess demonstrable demand, operational safety, total support burden, sustainable income, legal and insurance obligations, and ownership of intellectual property before incorporation or hiring.

## Transparency

Keep donations distinct from paid contracts; explicitly state paid entitlements and support response targets when introduced. A sponsor does not automatically become a paid customer.
